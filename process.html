Agile SDLC & Sprint Methodology

Predictable Engineering. Zero Black Boxes.

A transparent six-stage process built around working software, clear priorities, and regular feedback.

01

Discovery & System Architecture

We align on goals, users, system boundaries, data, integrations, and security needs before implementation begins.

Deliverable: Architecture plan and agreed scope
02

Sprint Planning & Backlog Grooming

We break the roadmap into prioritized stories, define acceptance criteria, and agree on sprint goals.

Deliverable: Prioritized sprint backlog
03

Modular Development & Peer Reviews

Engineers build in small increments, document decisions, and review changes before they are integrated.

Deliverable: Reviewable, working increments
04

Quality Assurance & Integration

We validate the agreed workflows, integrations, and product behavior as features are completed.

Deliverable: QA findings and resolved issues
05

Cloud Deployment

We prepare the release, deploy to the agreed environment, and check service health after launch.

Deliverable: Deployed release and handover
06

Sprint Demo & Retrospective

We review completed work with your team, collect feedback, and use it to shape the next iteration.

Deliverable: Demo, decisions, and next sprint priorities
